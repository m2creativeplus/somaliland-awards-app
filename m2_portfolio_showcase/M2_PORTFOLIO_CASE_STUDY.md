## 1. Executive Summary

The **Somaliland National Awards Platform** is an enterprise-grade digital government system designed and engineered by **M2 Creative & Consulting**. The platform modernizes national recognition, public voting, and award management for Somaliland. Built with Next.js 14, Payload CMS, Prisma, and TailwindCSS, it delivers a secure, accessible, and high-impact visual identity aligned with Somaliland’s national colors (Emerald Green `#1B5E20`, Gold `#CFA93F`, Red `#C62828`).

## 2. Institutional & Strategic Objectives

* **Public Recognition**: Establish a transparent, national portal for nominating leaders, innovators, artists, and public servants.
* **Integrity & Security**: Implement verified voting mechanisms, jury evaluation dashboards, and administrative oversight.
* **Digital Excellence**: Serve as a lighthouse digital government project representing Somaliland's tech vision for 2035.

---

## 3. Core Architecture & Tech Stack

* **Frontend Engine**: Next.js 14 (App Router) with SSR/SSG pre-rendering.
* **CMS & Content Engine**: Payload CMS 3.x embedded with Lexical Rich Text.
* **Database & ORM**: SQLite / PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM 6.x.
* **Design System**: Somaliland National Design System (OKLCH green/gold tokens, glassmorphism, micro-animations).

### Core User Journeys

1. **Homepage (`/`)**: Hero section featuring the Somaliland Tri-color Accent Bar, active stats, and category highlights.
2. **Awards Directory (`/awards`)**: Interactive category grid with criteria, past winners, and current nominees.
3. **Public Voting (`/vote`)**: Real-time ballot card interface with vote count constraints and security verification.
4. **Nomination Portal (`/submit`)**: Multi-step form with document upload, category selection, and validation.
5. **Jury Evaluation Portal (`/jury`)**: Scorecard matrix and candidate dossier review interface.
6. **Admin Suite (`/admin`)**: Analytics dashboard, submission oversight, user role control, and system settings.
